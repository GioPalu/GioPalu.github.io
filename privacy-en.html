OnTrack privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who is responsible

OnTrack is an app developed by Giovanni Palu. Contact: palugio96@gmail.com.

What data the app uses

  • Use without an account (optional): the app creates an anonymous user, with no email or name, which only recognizes that device. Google or Microsoft services are not used until the user links an account; when an account is linked, the data already entered moves to that account.
  • Google account: email address, name and profile picture, to identify the user.
  • Google Calendar: list of calendars and events of the chosen calendar (title, times, color). Normally the app only reads them. With the optional edit permission (which the user grants to Google only if they wish, from Settings), the app can create an event, move it (day, start and end) or delete it ("Edit the calendar from the app", "Delete events from the app"), always and only when the user asks for that event, and only on calendars they own. Events it creates get the title and color chosen by the user; in other events it does not change the title, color or any other data.
  • Google Drive (only files created by the app): the app creates an "OnTrack" folder and saves backup files there. It cannot see or change the other files in Drive.
  • Microsoft account (optional, to sign in to the app or link Outlook): email address and profile name, to identify the user.
  • Outlook calendar: list of calendars, categories with their color and events of the chosen calendars (title, times, category). The same rules as Google Calendar apply: normally read-only; creating, moving or deleting a single event only when the user asks for it, if they have turned on the related options.
  • OneDrive (only the app folder): the app saves backup files in its "Apps/OnTrack" folder. It cannot see or change the other files in OneDrive.
  • Data entered in the app: start and end times of the activities carried out, events created only in the app ("OnTrack only": title, activity, times) and settings.
  • Language and region: language, country, date and time format, first day of the week and time zone chosen in the app, to show the app in the right language and count days and times in the user's time zone.
  • Backup on the device (optional): the app saves backup files in the device folder chosen by the user and reads only the files the user chooses for a restore. These files stay on the device and the app does not send them to anyone (for a restore, the content of the chosen file is sent to the OnTrack server to replace the user's data).

How it is used

The data is used only to make the app work: showing the day, sending notifications, calculating statistics and making backups. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties. The same applies to the data received from Microsoft (Microsoft Graph).

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

The data is stored on Supabase (servers in the European Union), protected so that each user can access only their own. Access credentials for Google and Microsoft are stored on the server in protected form and are never visible to other users. Backups are in the user's Google Drive and/or OneDrive and/or in a folder on their device, according to their choice.

How to delete the data

  • In the app: Settings → Account → "Delete account and data" permanently deletes the account and all the data kept by OnTrack. Without an account, "Sign out" also deletes all the data on the server.
  • Revoke OnTrack's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Revoke OnTrack's access to your Microsoft account at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or at myapps.microsoft.com (work or school accounts).
  • Ask for all your data to be deleted by writing to palugio96@gmail.com.
  • Backups can be deleted by the user at any time, by deleting the "OnTrack" folder on Google Drive, "Apps/OnTrack" on OneDrive or the backup files in the chosen folder on the device.
